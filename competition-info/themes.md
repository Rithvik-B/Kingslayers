# ContextCon 03: IIT Bombay — the themes, in full

Text of `ContextCon 03- IIT Bombay · The Themes.pptx` (organizer deck, "show during idea time").
Converted so every teammate's Claude can read it. The .pptx is the source of truth if they differ.

> Or your own idea: **any service people are paid to do, on data about people and companies.**

Each theme ends with a "We're looking for" line. That line describes how entries will be judged, so plan
for it from the start.

---

## 1. Hiring

**The job today:** Recruiters and staffing agencies are paid when someone starts work, not for a list of
names. The person who fits rarely applies, often goes by another title, and changed jobs weeks before their
profile says so.

**Build an agent that fills roles with no one at the keyboard:**
- Take a three-line brief to a signed offer, while keeping a hundred conversations alive for weeks.
- Prove or disprove every claim on a candidate's record.
- Staff a thousand shifts from applications seeded with fakes.

**We're looking for:** agents that, tested on last year's real job openings, find the people who were
actually hired.

## 2. Investing

**The job today:** Venture funds and banks pay analysts to find companies, check them, track them and price
them. Every call is made on what is known that day, and whether it was right shows only years later.

**Build an agent that makes a fund's calls on its own:**
- Back founders before their first round.
- Find what a startup's data room hides.
- See a portfolio company running out of cash before its founder says so.
- Rebuild a fund manager's real returns, deal by deal.
- Run a company's sale against buyers who bluff.
- Price an IPO from its 400-page offer document.

**We're looking for:** calls that hold up when tested on past dates, using only what was known on each date.

## 3. Research and markets

**The job today:** Analysts at banks, funds and research labs read, forecast and decide what to test next.
There is more to read than any person or model can hold at once, and a model trained on old news already
knows how past quarters ended.

**Build an agent that gets to the answer before the market does:**
- Answer any question about a company in an industry it has never seen, from thousands of pages, with the
  page behind every number.
- Forecast a company's next quarter before it reports, and trade on it.
- Spend a lab's fixed budget on the experiments most likely to find something new.

**We're looking for:** results scored on real numbers published after every model you use was trained.

## 4. Insurance

**The job today:** Underwriters, brokers and claims adjusters work out what a business really does, what it
needs to be covered for and what a claim is worth, mostly from what the customer chooses to tell them.

**Build an agent that insures a business with no one in the loop:**
- Check an insurance application against what outside data says about the business.
- Find the risks its 40-page policy leaves uncovered, and get quotes to fix them.
- Settle a damage claim from photos that may be edited, reused or lifted from the web.

**We're looking for:** decisions that cite their reasons and stay the same however the case is worded.

## 5. Trust and compliance

**The job today:** Compliance teams, credit analysts, auditors and fraud investigators decide who is real, who
is safe and who is lying. The evidence is spread across government registries, scanned annual reports and the
files of other lenders, which they may not see.

**Build an agent that decides who to trust:**
- Tell a trading business from a shell whose papers are all genuine.
- Set a credit limit from annual reports in any language.
- Flag the filing a company will later have to correct.
- Separate a fraud ring from companies that simply share an office.
- Let lenders warn each other about fraud without revealing a single customer.

**We're looking for:** every fake caught and no honest business turned away.

## 6. Buying and paying

**The job today:** Procurement and payables teams decide which supplier gets an order and which invoice gets
paid, and they are the last check before money leaves the company.

**Build an agent you can hand the company's books:**
- Turn a vague purchase request into a supplier choice an auditor would sign off on.
- Pay every invoice exactly once, even if the payment run crashes halfway.

**We're looking for:** zero payments made in error.

## 7. Agents that spend

**The job today:** People and companies want agents that pay for what a task needs, from a laptop or a
software plan to a dataset or an hour of compute. Today the choice is a person approving every payment, or an
agent trusted with an open card.

**Build an agent that spends on someone's behalf with no one watching:**
- Buy exactly what a person asked for, from shops it has never seen.
- Sign a team up for the tools a project needs, and keep the bill right for months.
- Keep a dozen agents inside one shared budget while sellers try to talk them past it.

**We're looking for:** jobs finished end to end, with every rupee inside limits the owner can read, change and
take back at any moment.

## 8. Supply chain

**The job today:** Freight forwarders and hardware buyers move goods and parts across borders between a dozen
companies that share no system, mostly by email, spreadsheet and supplier portal.

**Build an agent that runs those operations end to end:**
- Take hundreds of shipments from price quote to paid invoice, while updates arrive late, out of order or
  twice.
- Land every part on a satellite's bill of materials, genuine and on time.
- Do a clerk's whole morning inside a website that has no API.

**We're looking for:** operations where every record, document and cost agrees at any moment.

## 9. Revenue

**The job today:** Sales, marketing, strategy and fundraising teams win by reaching the right person at the
right moment, and both change every week.

**Build an agent that brings in revenue on its own:**
- Reach the person who decides, the week their company starts looking to buy.
- Answer and route every inbound lead within a minute, without inventing a claim.
- Forecast a rival's next launch from who it is hiring.
- Decide which of tens of thousands of alumni to ask for a gift, when and for how much.

**We're looking for:** meetings booked, gifts given and forecasts that come true, more of them every run.

## 10. Infrastructure for agents

**The job today:** Every agent in the other themes needs the same building blocks, and most of them don't
exist yet.

**Build one of them:**
- Memory that carries hundreds of cases for months.
- A company's rules and know-how, turned into checks agents run and cite.
- CRM, billing and support systems that keep their records in agreement on their own.
- A map of who knows whom, scored by how closely they worked.
- Proof of who sent a message, and on whose authority an agent acts.
- A check that stops a wrong email before it is sent.
- A tamper-proof record of where every fact came from.
- An exam built from facts no model has seen.

**We're looking for:** building blocks that still hold after a crash, a model swap or a lie.
